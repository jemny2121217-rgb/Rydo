WWII FRONTLINE

Defend the battlefield.
Move, aim, shoot and survive.

What you should see When you open it, you'll get WWII FRONTLINE → START BATTLE. After pressing START BATTLE: - 🪖 Your soldier appears in the middle. - 🔴 Enemy soldiers continuously approach. - 🎯 Aim toward the enemy. - 🔫 FIRE shoots. - 🔄 RELOAD restores ammunition. - ❤️ Your health decreases when enemies reach you. - 🎯 Killing enemies increases your score. - 💥 Enemies produce explosion particles when killed. - 💀 When health reaches zero, MISSION FAILED appears. - 🔁 PLAY AGAIN starts another match. This is still a single-player enemy-shooter, not true online PvP yet. Once this base is working on your phone, the next step is to add the actual two-player online connection rather than pretending local buttons are online multiplayer.
